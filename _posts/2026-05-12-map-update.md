OpenStreetMap data as of May 7, 2026 is now available on Wandrer. If you're upgraded, you can request the update on your [settings page](https://wandrer.earth/settings). You can also request to keep your map data updated, but this means only that you'll get updated eventually, not soon.

If you're not upgraded, you can request an update for $5.
